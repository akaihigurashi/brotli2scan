brotli2scan

Is your website affected by HTTP2Shell?

HTTP2Shell (CVE-2026-111497) is a critical bug in the Brotli decompression library. Attackers can crash servers or run code on them by sending crafted Brotli data. It affects Node.js, Python, .NET, Envoy and more. Enter your website to see which of them it runs on.

The check loads your start page the same way a browser does. Abuse may be reported to the authorities.

Why the bread?

Brötli is Swiss German for a small bread roll. Google's engineers in Zurich named the Brotli compression format after it. Now there's a bug in it, so our scanner got a bread roll too.

How it works

We load the start page of your website and look for the software it runs on: Node.js, Bun, .NET, Envoy or Python, and frameworks that only run on them. With the test request ticked, we also check whether your server unpacks Brotli data sent to it.

Rules of use

Only check websites you own or have permission to check. Abuse may be reported to the authorities.

Privacy

For every check we store the time, your IP address, your browser's name and the checked address, to prevent and investigate abuse. These records are deleted after 30 days, unless they are needed to report or investigate a specific case of misuse. No cookies, no tracking, no third-party content.

brotli2scan is part of shouldipanic.ch, open-source checks for current web vulnerabilities.
